Vancouver Dining

  • Top of Vancouver Revolving Restaurant Fine dining 553 feet above the city with panoramic 360-degree views of Vancouver, the coastal mountains, and the ocean.
  • JOEY (Coquitlam, Burrard, or Burnaby) Upscale casual dining featuring globally inspired dishes, handcrafted cocktails, and a vibrant atmosphere across multiple Metro Vancouver locations.
  • Ask For Luigi An intimate, highly sought-after Railtown eatery famous for its handmade fresh pasta and comforting Italian dishes.

Granville Island

  • The Vancouver Fish Company Waterfront dining specializing in fresh, locally sourced Pacific Northwest seafood and ocean views.
  • Tap & Barrel A lively waterfront spot offering locally brewed craft beers, BC wines on tap, and elevated comfort food.
  • The Lobster Man An iconic Granville Island seafood market and counter-service spot famous for fresh lobster rolls and live Atlantic lobster.

Whistler Dining

  • Balam Modern Mexican cuisine and vibrant cocktails located in the heart of Whistler Village.
  • Araxi Restaurant + Oyster Bar A renowned Whistler Village cornerstone celebrated for fresh Pacific Northwest seafood, farm-to-table dining, and an extensive wine list.
  • Bar Oso An intimate, stylish lounge offering Spanish-influenced tapas, house-made charcuterie, and craft cocktails.
  • The Wildflower (Upper Village) A welcoming, full-service restaurant in the Upper Village highlighting regional Pacific Northwest cuisine and locally sourced ingredients.
  • Nagomi Sushi (Upper Village) A lively Upper Village restaurant serving fresh sushi rolls, sashimi, and Japanese comfort dishes right at the base of Blackcomb Mountain.
  • Portobello (Upper Village) A bustling, casual café in the Upper Village famous for fresh-baked pastries, specialty coffee, and made-to-order smoked meat sandwiches.
  • Rimrock Café A cozy, lodge-style favorite tucked away in Creekside, famous for refined seafood and game dishes.
  • Peaked Pies A popular Australian-style bakery café serving savory meat pies, sweet pastries, and coffee—ideal for a quick bite after hitting the slopes.
